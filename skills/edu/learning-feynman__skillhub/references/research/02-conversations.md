## 1. BBC《发现的乐趣》(1981)

### 访谈背景
- **节目**: BBC Horizon / PBS Nova
- **时间**: 1981年11月播出
- **主持人**: Christopher Sykes
- **意义**: 费曼最全面的人生回顾访谈之一

### 核心对话摘录

#### 关于父亲的教导
> "My father used to sit me on his lap, and the one book we did use all the time was the Encyclopedia Britannica... He would always stop and he would say, 'You know what that means?' He would translate everything."

> "A thing that was very important about my father was not the facts but the process. How we find out."

#### 关于广岛原子弹
> "The only reaction that I remember... was a very considerable elation and excitement... and there were parties and people got drunk... at the same time as people were dying and struggling in Hiroshima."

> "I realized... to drop one [bomb] on 34th Street, it would spread all the way out here and all these people would be killed... things were sort of doomed."

#### 关于诺贝尔奖
> "I don't know anything about the Nobel Prize, I don't understand what it's all about... I won't have anything to do with the Nobel Prize... it's a pain in the..."

> "The prize is the pleasure of finding the thing out, the kick in the discovery, the observation that other people use it [my work] — those are the real things, the honors are unreal to me."

#### 关于荣誉
> "I don't believe in honors, it bothers me, honors is epaulettes, honors is uniforms."

#### 关于战后心理
> "I haven't done anything important and I'm never going to do anything important. But I used to enjoy physics and mathematical things... and because I used to play with them it was in very short order that I worked the things out for which I later won the Nobel Prize."

## 2. 曼哈顿计划经历

### 加入动机
> "It would mean that I would have to stop the research in what I was doing, which is my life's desire, to take time off to do this, which I felt I should do in order to protect civilization."

> "Since it might be possible, it must be possible. There was nothing that I knew that indicated that if we could do it they couldn't do it."

### 道德反思
> "What I did — immorally I would say — was to not remember the reason that I said I was doing it, so that when the reason changed... not the single thought came to my mind at all about that."

> "I simply didn't think, okay?"

### 洛斯阿拉莫斯生活
> "I was involved with this happy thing and also drinking and drunk and playing drums sitting on the hood of a Jeep... at the same time as people were dying and struggling in Hiroshima."

### 与玻尔的对话
费曼在回忆中描述了与尼尔斯·玻尔(Niels Bohr)的讨论：
> 玻尔经常在深夜单独约见费曼讨论物理问题。费曼后来才知道这是因为玻尔认为费曼是唯一敢于挑战他观点的人。

## 3. 挑战者号事故调查

### 调查背景
- **时间**: 1986年
- **角色**: 罗杰斯委员会成员(唯一非NASA成员)

### 关键对话与发现

#### 关于O型环的演示
在电视直播中，费曼将O型环样品放入冰水，展示了橡胶在低温下失去弹性的现象：

> "I took this stuff that I got out of your seal and I put it in ice water, and I discovered that when you put some pressure on it for a while and then undo it, it maintains, it doesn't stretch back."

> "In other words... there's no resilience in this particular material when it's at a temperature of 32 degrees Fahrenheit. I believe that has some significance to our problem."

#### 关于NASA的风险评估
费曼发现NASA管理层声称故障率为1/100,000（即每天发射一次连续274年才发生一次事故），他对此表示强烈质疑：

> "The estimates range from roughly 1 in 100 to 1 in 100,000. The higher figures come from the working engineers..."

他私下调查工程师，得到的大多数估计是1/50到1/200（实际航天飞机事故率为1/68，两次事故/135次飞行）。

#### 关于安全系数
> "If a 1,000-pound truck drove across the bridge and a crack appeared in a beam, even just a third of the way through a beam, the safety factor is now zero."

#### 关于NASA文化
费曼拒绝在委员会报告上签字，最终其反对意见被收录为附录F。他威胁说：
> "For a successful technology, reality must take precedence over public relations, for nature cannot be fooled."

## 4. 与其他物理学家的对话

### 与汉斯·贝特(Hans Bethe)
- 战后加入康奈尔大学贝特的团队
- 费曼称贝特为"活着的最好的物理学家"

### 与约翰·惠勒(John Wheeler)的合作
关于"延迟选择实验"(Delayed Choice Experiment)的讨论，费曼回忆惠勒如何引导他思考：
> 惠勒在讲座中临时推导，而非预先准备，这让费曼意识到有时候教授可以从学生那里学到东西。

### 与朱利安·施温格(Julian Schwinger)的对比

| 维度 | 费曼 | 施温格 |
|------|------|--------|
| 方法 | 直观、图形化、形象思维 | 数学严谨、系统推导 |
| 表达 | 故事化、类比、通俗 | 抽象、形式化 |
| 教学 | 本科生能听懂 | 研究生水平 |
| 学生 | 很少带博士生 | 70多位博士生 |
| 态度 | 讨厌费曼图 | 完全禁止在课堂上使用 |

> "Schwinger was more mathematically inclined and heavily favoured mathematical rigour... Feynman was more intuitive and this showed in his Feynman diagram approach." — ScientificLib

### 与朝永振一郎(Sin-Itiro Tomonaga)
- 三人因量子电动力学(QED)共同获得1965年诺贝尔奖
- 费曼独立发展出与施温格、朝永类似但不同的方法

## 5. Key Conversation Patterns

### 费曼的对话风格特征

#### 直接挑战与质疑
- 不接受"专家说"而不验证
- 要求对方从基础开始解释
- "Start from bedrock"

#### 使用类比和故事
- 将抽象概念转化为具体场景
- 如解释光的双缝实验时使用"光子糖果"比喻

#### 承认无知
- 面对记者关于磁铁问题的追问时说：
  > "I can't explain that attraction in terms of anything else that's familiar to you... I'm trying to explain it in terms of something more familiar, and that's cheating."

- BBC"Fun to Imagine"系列(1983)中关于磁力的经典对话：
  > "Why do magnets attract? I can't explain that in terms of something else familiar to you... I don't understand it either."

#### 自嘲式幽默
- 回忆自己计算错误时：
  > "I was stupid... what I had described and calculated was just ordinary reflected light, not radiation reaction."

- 关于诺贝尔奖：
  > "I could have found out later in the morning." (凌晨3点被叫醒得知获奖)
