# Business Administration - Thesis Writing Guide

## Overview
Business administration thesis focuses on practical value, combining theory with practice. Core assessment criteria: problem relevance, theoretical foundation, methodology rigor, data reliability, and conclusion practicality.

## Research Types

### 1. Case Study
- **Features**: Deep analysis of specific companies or industries
- **Structure**: Case background → Problem identification → Analysis framework → In-depth analysis → Insights and recommendations
- **Key Points**: Case selection should be representative, analysis should be thorough

### 2. Empirical Research
- **Features**: Verify theories or hypotheses using data
- **Structure**: Literature review → Hypothesis development → Research design → Data analysis → Results discussion
- **Key Points**: Data sources should be reliable, methods should be appropriate

### 3. Strategic Analysis
- **Features**: Formulate development strategies for enterprises
- **Structure**: Environmental analysis → Internal assessment → Strategy formulation → Implementation plan → Risk control
- **Key Points**: Strategy should be feasible, implementation should be detailed

## Chapter Structure

### Chapter 1: Introduction (8-10 pages)
1. Research background and practical significance
2. Problem statement (management pain points)
3. Research objectives and questions
4. Research methodology overview
5. Thesis structure

### Chapter 2: Literature Review (12-15 pages)
1. Theoretical foundations (relevant management theories)
2. Research history (domestic and international)
3. Current research status
4. Research gaps (where this study fits)

### Chapter 3: Research Design (8-10 pages)
1. Research framework
2. Hypothesis development (if applicable)
3. Variable definitions and measurement
4. Data collection methods
5. Analysis methods

### Chapter 4: Case/Empirical Analysis (15-20 pages)
1. Case background or data description
2. Analysis process (step-by-step)
3. Key findings (with data support)
4. Result interpretation

### Chapter 5: Conclusions and Recommendations (5-8 pages)
1. Main research conclusions
2. Management implications
3. Policy recommendations
4. Research limitations
5. Future research directions

## Writing Standards

### Data Presentation
- Data sources must be clearly stated
- Include sample size, time period, collection methods
- Process raw data appropriately (do not fabricate)
- Use charts to enhance readability

### Theoretical Application
- Do not pile up theories; select relevant ones
- Theory should guide analysis, not just decorate the paper
- Build your own analysis framework if possible

### Practical Recommendations
- Recommendations should be specific and actionable
- Avoid empty slogans
- Provide implementation steps and timeline
- Consider feasibility and cost

## Common Issues

1. **Weak theoretical foundation**: Pure experience sharing, no theoretical support
2. **Insufficient data**: Small sample size, single case, low credibility
3. **Shallow analysis**: Staying at the description level, no deep insight
4. **Vague recommendations**: General and empty, not implementable

## Recommended Tools
- SPSS/Stata (statistical analysis)
- NVivo (qualitative analysis)
- Excel (data processing)
- Zotero (literature management)

## Citation Format
- APA format (common in business field)
- Prioritize top-tier journals (UTD24, FT50)
- Balance between classic and latest literature

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Is the management problem clearly identified?
- [ ] Is the theoretical foundation solid?
- [ ] Is the data reliable and sufficient?
- [ ] Is the analysis deep and insightful?
- [ ] Are the recommendations practical?
- [ ] Is the writing professional and logical?
