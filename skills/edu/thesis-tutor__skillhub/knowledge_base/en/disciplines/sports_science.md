# Sports Science Thesis Writing Guide

## Discipline Characteristics
- Emphasis on sports practice, scientific training, health promotion
- Strong focus on experimental research, survey research, practical application
- Diverse research methods: experiments, tests, surveys, cases
- Strong interdisciplinary nature (medicine, education, management, engineering)

## Research Types

### 1. Sports Training Research
- **Applicable to**: Training methods, fitness testing, tactical analysis
- **Key elements**:
  - Training programs (periodization, load, recovery)
  - Test indicators (heart rate, lactic acid, VO2max)
  - Effect evaluation (performance, fitness, skills)
- **Thesis structure**: Objective → Method → Experiment → Results → Discussion

### 2. Exercise Science Research
- **Applicable to**: Exercise physiology, sports medicine, sports biomechanics
- **Key elements**:
  - Experimental design (control, randomization, double-blind)
  - Measurement methods (instruments, indicators)
  - Data analysis (statistics, comparison)
- **Thesis structure**: Introduction → Methods → Results → Discussion → Conclusion

### 3. Physical Education Research
- **Applicable to**: Teaching methods, curriculum design, student development
- **Key elements**:
  - Teaching experiment (experimental group, control group)
  - Effect measurement (skills, knowledge, attitudes)
  - Teaching reflection (problems, improvements)
- **Thesis structure**: Background → Design → Implementation → Effect → Reflection

### 4. Sports Sociology Research
- **Applicable to**: Sports policy, sports industry, sports culture
- **Key elements**:
  - Survey design (questionnaires, interviews)
  - Data collection (samples, sampling)
  - Analysis methods (statistics, qualitative)
- **Thesis structure**: Problem → Literature → Method → Survey → Conclusion

## Discipline Subdivisions

### Sports Training Science
- **Physical training**: Strength, speed, endurance, flexibility
- **Technical training**: Movement analysis, technical diagnosis, training methods
- **Tactical training**: Game analysis, tactical design, psychological training
- **Training monitoring**: Physiological indicators, load monitoring, recovery evaluation

### Exercise Science
- **Exercise physiology**: Cardiopulmonary function, metabolism, endocrinology
- **Sports medicine**: Injury prevention, rehabilitation, medical supervision
- **Sports biomechanics**: Movement analysis, mechanical modeling
- **Sports nutrition**: Energy metabolism, nutritional supplementation, weight control

### Physical Education
- **PE teaching**: Teaching methods, teaching evaluation, curriculum design
- **School sports**: Extracurricular activities, physical health, sunshine sports
- **PE teachers**: Professional development, teaching ability, professional quality

### Sports Management
- **Sports industry**: Market analysis, business management, sports marketing
- **Sports policy**: Policy analysis, institutional reform, public services
- **Sports organizations**: Club management, event management, human resources

## Thesis Structure Template

### Experimental Research Type
```
1. Introduction (background, objectives, significance)
2. Research Subjects and Methods (subjects, design, indicators, statistics)
3. Results (data, charts)
4. Discussion (analysis, comparison, mechanisms)
5. Conclusion (main findings, recommendations)
```

### Survey Research Type
```
1. Introduction (problem background, research objectives)
2. Research Design (subjects, methods, tools)
3. Survey Results (data, charts)
4. Analysis and Discussion (findings, comparison, explanation)
5. Conclusion and Recommendations (main viewpoints, improvement directions)
```

## Writing Tips

### Experimental Design
- Sample selection (age, gender, training level)
- Grouping methods (random, matched)
- Intervention program (intensity, frequency, duration)
- Test indicators (validity, reliability)

### Data Presentation
- Physiological indicators (heart rate, blood lactate, VO2max)
- Fitness indicators (strength, speed, endurance)
- Statistical methods (t-test, ANOVA, correlation)
- Chart standards (error bars, significance markers)

### Practical Value
- Training recommendations (targeted, actionable)
- Teaching improvements (methods, content, evaluation)
- Policy recommendations (feasible, effective)

## Common Issues

1. **Insufficient sample size**: Low statistical power, unreliable conclusions
2. **Loose experimental control**: Many confounding factors, unclear causality
3. **Improper indicator selection**: Indicators insensitive or irrelevant
4. **Superficial data analysis**: Only descriptive statistics, lack of deep analysis
5. **Unclear practical value**: Recommendations vague, lack of operability

- Data analysis must be original
- Conclusions must be based on your own results
- Training recommendations require targeted content
