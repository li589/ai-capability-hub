## Description: <br>
Generates consistent comic art-direction assets and AI drawing prompts for characters, scenes, and storyboard frames. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[samwang-001](https://clawhub.ai/user/samwang-001) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Comic creators, storyboard artists, and AI art prompt engineers use this skill to define reusable character and scene assets, generate per-shot prompts, and keep a unified visual style across comic frames. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation wording may trigger the skill for general art or design requests beyond comic storyboard prompt generation. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke the skill explicitly or narrow the trigger wording when installing it in environments that should limit activation to comic storyboard prompt generation. <br>
Risk: Generated drawing prompts may contain incorrect, inconsistent, or unsuitable visual details when the storyboard or character inputs are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated character assets, scene descriptions, positive prompts, negative prompts, and tool parameters against the skill's quality checklist before sending them to image-generation tools. <br>
