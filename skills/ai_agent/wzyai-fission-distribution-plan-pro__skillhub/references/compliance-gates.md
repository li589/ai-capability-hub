# Compliance Gate

## Governing principle

Assess economic substance, not the marketing label or number of levels. “二级” or “不超过三级” is not an automatic safe harbor. The baseline authority is the Chinese Ministry of Justice administrative regulation database entry for the `禁止传销条例`, especially Articles 2 and 7: <https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=148&Query=>.

“二级”“三级以内”“佣金来自货款”均不是当然合规证明。判断经济实质是否存在付费资格、强制购买、招募计酬、上下线关系和下线业绩返佣；任一高风险要素不得因层级少或资金来自商品交易而被忽略。

## Green

- Commission comes only from the promoter's own genuine completed retail transaction.
- No paid entry qualification, forced stock purchase, recruitment pay, or downline pay.
- Refund reversals, settlement timing, tax handling, and prohibited claims are disclosed.
- Non-cash recognition is not linked to recruiting people or downline performance.

Continue, while retaining sector, advertising, tax, privacy, consumer, and platform checks.

## Yellow

- Purchase threshold, stock pressure, deposit, training fee, automatic renewal, team-performance reward, earnings display, ambiguous attribution, or high refund exposure.
- Missing permits, labels, ad approvals, platform permission, or regional operating facts.

Stop public output. Identify the exact uncertainty, request evidence, and offer a more conservative structure. Continue only after the facts are resolved and current official rules are checked.

## Red

- 按招募人数计酬。
- 交费取得发展资格，或以认购商品变相取得资格。
- 按下线业绩给上线返利。
- Rolling multi-level pay, sham transactions, self-buy arbitrage, or guaranteed/fixed returns.
- Medical cure claims, fabricated cases, or public earnings claims without lawful substantiation.

Do not create the requested executable mechanism, recruitment script, poster, or image. Replace it with own-order direct commission, a compliant service fee, or a non-cash incentive detached from recruitment and downline performance.

## Required response by level

| Level | Response |
|---|---|
| Green | Generate with disclosures and review checklist |
| Yellow | Name uncertainty, request evidence, research official rules, propose safer alternative |
| Red | Refuse operational details and public material; provide a safer architecture |

## Regulated industries

For health, medical, food, cosmetics, finance, insurance, education, employment, real estate, and similar sectors, verify sector permits, advertising restrictions, mandatory notices, platform rules, and regional applicability before generating claims or public images.

食品与大健康场景必须逐项核验经营资质、产品标签、食品广告、营养声称、减脂表达和疾病预防或治疗宣称。资质或适用规则未核实前，相关内容保持黄色闸门，不得生成对外功效文案。
