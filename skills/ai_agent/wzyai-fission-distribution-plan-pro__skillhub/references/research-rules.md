# Research and Citation Rules

## Research targets

Research only facts that materially affect design: price/cost ranges, real cases, platform rules, sector restrictions, current regulation, permits, tax/settlement constraints, and measurable benchmarks.

## Source priority

1. Government, legislature, judiciary, or regulator for law and regulation.
2. Official platform rule center for platform requirements.
3. Company filings, announcements, or official materials for company facts.
4. Primary datasets or traceable industry reports for market ranges.
5. Reputable secondary analysis only when primary evidence is unavailable; lower confidence explicitly.

Do not cite search-result pages. Do not use a single competitor price as a market average. Use at least two independent observations for a range when practical.

## Citation contract

For every time-sensitive conclusion record:

- claim
- source title
- direct URL
- publisher
- publication/effective date when available
- query date
- geography and scope
- confidence (`高`, `中`, `低`)

Place citations next to the claim and repeat them in the report's source list. Quote minimally; paraphrase unless exact wording is necessary.

## Confidence and conflicts

Separate user facts, file facts, external facts, estimates, and advice. When official sources conflict, prefer the source with direct jurisdiction and current effective status; explain the conflict. When evidence is insufficient, say so and use a range or request professional review instead of inventing a precise answer.

## Unsupported-claim gate

- 无法核实的白皮书、案例、转化率、行业倍数和安全线不得写成事实。
- 单个竞品价格不得代表行业均价；市场范围尽量使用两个独立观察值。
- 不能定位真实主体和可靠来源的案例必须改写为假设性打法。
- 每个时效性结论附直接链接、发布或生效日期、查询日期、地区和适用平台。
