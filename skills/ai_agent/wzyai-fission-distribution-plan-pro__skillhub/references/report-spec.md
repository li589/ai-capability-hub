# Report Contract

## Internal report

Use these sections in order:

1. 执行摘要与是否值得启动
2. 战役主题与一句话增长战略
3. 已确认事实、外部证据和待验证假设
4. 产品分销适配性
5. 两类目标分销人群的深度画像
6. 五维能力评估及证据缺口
7. 合规角色、交易路径和结算条件
8. 三档直接销售激励模型
9. 非现金成长与认可体系
10. 引流品、内容矩阵和裂变路径
11. 前100名种子分销员招募SOP
12. 培训、素材、社群和日常运营机制
13. 30/60/90天计划、负责人和指标
14. 财务敏感性、风险及停止条件
15. 管理层决策、待确认事项和来源清单

Lead with the decision, not role-play. Make actions, owners, timing, metrics, assumptions, and stop conditions explicit. A real case must have a verifiable source; otherwise label it as a hypothetical pattern.

## Action contract

每个关键建议必须明确：为什么做、对谁做、谁负责、什么时候做、用什么指标判断、什么情况下停止。缺少任一字段时，将建议降级为待补充项，不得包装成可直接执行的结论。

## External recruitment version

Include only approved public facts: product value, suitable partner profile, participation conditions, direct-sales rights, non-cash growth system, training/support, process, disclaimers, and restrained call to action. Exclude cost, margin, total budget, internal thresholds, sensitive risk judgments, projected earnings, and unverified performance claims.

## Financial scenarios

Provide `保守`, `基准`, and `进取` scenarios. For each show price, hard cost, 履约物流, 退款预留, 支付及平台费用, direct commission, operating reserve, tax reserve, company contribution, orders needed for the target, active distributors needed, conversion assumption, refund assumption, and sensitivity notes.

The allocation must close to price and satisfy `commission_rate <= incentive_budget_rate`. Use ranges for uncertain values. Never promise distributor income. If required inputs do not produce a non-negative viable model, state the gap and do not recommend a commission rate.
