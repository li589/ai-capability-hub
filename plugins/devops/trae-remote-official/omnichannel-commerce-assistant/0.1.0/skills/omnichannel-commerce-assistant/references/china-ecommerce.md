# China-domestic e-commerce

Use this reference only for mainland-China consumer commerce.

## Shared checks

- Separate platform subsidy, merchant discount, ad spend, commission, service fee, refund, and logistics cost.
- Distinguish search, recommendation, content/live, paid, affiliate/creator, and private-domain traffic.
- Account for platform event phases, stock availability, fulfillment promise, and refund/after-sales loss.
- Verify current platform rules and calendars from official sources; do not rely on remembered details.
- For content-commerce bestseller, creator, brand, or competitor benchmarking, read `research-tools.md` and choose current intelligence sources according to coverage and ranking definition.

## Platform lenses

| Channel | Emphasize |
|---|---|
| Taobao/Tmall | Search and recommendation mix, store membership, campaign mechanics, SKU structure, paid traffic economics |
| JD | Fulfillment promise, price competitiveness, search/category position, service cost, platform/self-operated distinction |
| Pinduoduo | Price-value perception, SKU configuration, activity exposure, subsidy source, supply response, sustainable contribution |
| Douyin/Kuaishou | Content/creator/live/paid mix, click-to-product and product-to-order conversion, refund quality, cohort value |
| Xiaohongshu | Discovery and consideration, seeding attribution limits, search capture, product credibility |
| WeChat/private domain | Reachable base, activity, conversion, contact cost, fatigue, membership value, consent/privacy |

## Common failure modes

- Treating event GMV as durable growth without post-event retention or margin.
- Comparing platforms without normalizing traffic source, subsidy, fulfillment, and refund basis.
- Calling low price optimal without contribution, quality, and return effects.
- Treating content exposure as equivalent to attributable incremental sales.
