# Cross-border e-commerce

Use this reference for a China-based or China-serving seller operating in an overseas market.

## Decision stack

Check in this order:

1. target country/site and customer need;
2. product eligibility, certification, labeling, IP, tax, and privacy;
3. landed contribution after product, freight, duty, tax, platform, fulfillment, ads, FX, returns, and storage;
4. channel fit and localization;
5. replenishment lead time, cash cycle, and exit options.

## Platform lenses

| Channel | Emphasize |
|---|---|
| Amazon | Search/ranking, Buy Box and offer quality, FBA/FBM economics, storage/returns, review policy, site-specific compliance |
| TikTok Shop | Content/affiliate/live mix, creator economics, fulfillment promise, refund quality, local content fit |
| Shopee/Lazada | Country-level traffic, marketplace events, local logistics/payment, price competitiveness, seller program costs |
| AliExpress/Temu/eBay/Walmart | Operating model, pricing control, service standard, logistics, account eligibility, current fee structure |
| DTC | Acquisition economics, conversion, payments, tax/privacy, localization, retention, first-party data quality |

## Landed economics

Use one currency and explicit FX date/rate. Show which costs are confirmed, estimated, or excluded. Stress-test sales price, ad cost, return rate, freight, duty/tax, and lead time. Do not use a marketplace fee as the whole cost stack.

## Localization and supply

Localization includes language, units, size, imagery, claims, payment, service expectations, seasonality, and returns—not translation alone. Evaluate local inventory, cross-border fulfillment, lead time, safety stock, minimum order quantity, aging risk, and cash tied in transit together.

## Common failure modes

- Choosing a country from headline market size without unit economics or eligibility.
- Copying China pricing/content assumptions into another market.
- Treating one country's platform rules as global.
- Scaling before a compliant, replenishable, contribution-positive pilot passes.
